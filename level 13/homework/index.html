Esdeekid

EsDeeKid is a British rapper from Liverpool, England. He released his debut album, Rebel, in June 2025. His music is characterised by his thick Scouse accent and draws inspiration from cloud rap. EsDeeKid's identity is unknown; he covers his face with a balaclava and keeps personal information, such as his age, private. He has mentioned on his Twitter that he still lives in his council house. As of January 2026, EsDeeKid's single "Phantom" (with Rico Ace) has accumulated more than 170 million streams. His album Rebel has charted in multiple European countries, as well as peaked at number 23 on the US Billboard 200, despite being released nearly five months prior to its debut on the chart. EsDeeKid has been described by Dazed as "the fastest-growing artist in the world right now".
